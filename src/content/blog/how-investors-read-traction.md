Revenue is the easiest number to put on a slide, and often the least informative one on its own. Two startups can show the same monthly revenue and be in completely different positions — one built on repeat customers who keep coming back, the other on a handful of one-time deals that won't recur.

Retention is usually the more honest signal. A cohort that keeps using the product month over month tells an investor the value proposition is real, independent of how much marketing spend is behind it.

The other underrated signal is how customers find the product. Organic, word-of-mouth growth — even at a smaller scale than paid acquisition — suggests the market is pulling the product forward rather than the founder pushing it out.
