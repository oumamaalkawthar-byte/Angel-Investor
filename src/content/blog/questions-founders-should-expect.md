The best pitch meetings feel less like a presentation and more like a stress test. Investors are trying to find the edges of the business — the assumptions that, if wrong, would change the entire investment case.

Expect to be asked what happens if your biggest customer leaves, what your unit economics look like at twice today's scale, and why now is the right time for this specific business rather than a year from now or a year ago.

The founders who handle these questions well aren't the ones with a perfect answer for everything — they're the ones who can say "we don't know yet, and here's how we're finding out" without it undermining the rest of the pitch.
