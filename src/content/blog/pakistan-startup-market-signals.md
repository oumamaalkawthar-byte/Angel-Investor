Pakistan's early-stage market moves in cycles that are easy to misread from a single quarter of headlines. Capital availability, sector rotation and public-market sentiment all shift the appetite for early-stage risk in ways that take a few months to become visible in deal data.

Fintech, logistics and B2B SaaS have consistently drawn the most consistent attention from local and regional investors, largely because they solve problems with clear, measurable unit economics — something that's easier to underwrite in a market where trust in projections still has to be earned deal by deal.

For founders, the practical takeaway isn't to chase whichever sector is currently fashionable — it's to understand which investors are actively deploying capital in your specific stage and sector right now, since that changes faster than the broader narrative suggests.
