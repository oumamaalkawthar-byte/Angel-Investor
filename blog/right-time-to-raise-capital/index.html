When is the right time to raise capital?

Raising capital is often treated as a milestone in itself, when it should be treated as a tool for a specific job. The right question isn’t “are we ready to raise” — it’s “what will this capital let us do that we genuinely cannot do without it.”

A useful test: write down exactly what the next 12–18 months of the business look like with the raise, and what they look like without it. If the answer is mostly “grow faster” without a specific new capability unlocked, it may be worth extending runway on current resources a little longer.

Capital raised too early, before the business has proven the thing investors are actually betting on, tends to buy time rather than progress — and that time runs out just as fast either way.
